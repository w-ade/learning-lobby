# Track D - Design Technologist

## Goal
12 two-week stages, modeled on Suraj Sharma's agentic-AI-engineer roadmap, rebuilt for design technology. Each stage has a goal, topics, free resources, a weekly rhythm, a must-build and a done-when list. Standing rule, prototype in code: ship one small build every week, and before calling it done, read every file the AI wrote and change one thing by hand.

Top target: Little Plains, Design Technologist. Contract role, Next.js/React/Claude Code. They want a resume, a portfolio and a note on how you use agentic tools. Stage 1 produces all three in 10 days. Everything after makes you stronger for the next ones.

Weights: scroll storytelling (Stages 4–5) and WebGL (6–7) get double time because they are your gaps. Stages that build on oklab, glide and dev-lines are shorter on paper.
